Product scope

Where NetConfiger fits

The upstream VPN client remains on the Windows host. NetConfiger provides explicit LAN proxy endpoints; compatible downstream apps use the host IP and port. This makes workstation setup, handover and troubleshooting more repeatable.

Scope: it does not transparently route an entire downstream PC, provide a Layer 2 bridge, or supply VPN accounts or proxy lines.
